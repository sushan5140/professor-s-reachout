I found NS Lab while looking through graph and LLM research, and your recent work using LLMs to build a disease knowledge graph caught my attention.

I’ve been building Minos-J around a simple habit: before trusting a generated answer, try to find the smallest change that makes its reasoning fail.

That made me wonder about LLM-assisted knowledge-graph construction.

If a model extracts a relation correctly from one wording of a document, does it keep the same relation when the same evidence is paraphrased, reordered, or contains one distracting sentence?

I’d start with a small set of relations with known source evidence, generate matched variants of that evidence, and compare extraction stability. I’d also like to see whether the model’s confidence actually drops when the evidence becomes ambiguous.

I’m not treating this as a finished proposal, and I may be missing a graph-specific verification method that already handles this better. I’d really value your opinion.
