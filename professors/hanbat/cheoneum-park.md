My name is SUSAN. I finished high school in India this year, and I’m preparing to apply for undergraduate study in AI/Computer Science in Korea through GKS.

I found your NLP Lab while looking through recent RAG and agent work, and DuoPRAG especially caught my attention.

I’ve been building an independent project called Minos-J where I keep trying to separate “the system got better” from “this particular mechanism actually caused the improvement.”

That made me curious about RAG systems where retrieval and reasoning are mixed together very tightly.

If a model improves after adding a retrieval component, I’d like to know whether the retrieved knowledge is really being used, or whether the reasoning path changed for some other reason.

A small experiment I had in mind is to take successful RAG examples and intervene on one part at a time: keep the retrieved passage but block its contribution to reasoning, keep the reasoning path but swap the evidence, or give the same evidence in a different retrieval order.

I’d want to see which changes actually move the answer, instead of only comparing final benchmark scores.

I may be describing something your current work already handles more cleanly, so I’d really value your opinion rather than treating this as a finished idea.
