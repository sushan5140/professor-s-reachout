I’m developing a small framework called Meno-J that treats evaluation as falsification: instead of only confirming a model’s headline performance, it searches for controlled cases that break the claim and then records what actually caused the failure.

I wondered whether that could be useful for multimodal or Video-LLM evaluation — for example, generating paired cases that keep superficial cues similar while changing motion direction, composition, temporal ordering, or another causal factor.
